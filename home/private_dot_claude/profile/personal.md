# Personal machine
A personal machine: no employer tooling, credentials or knowledge bases are installed here.

## Clones
Main clones are grouped by owner: `~/src/<owner>/<repo>`, such as `~/src/prakashsurya/meta` and `~/src/velvet-alchemy/meta`, so each account's and organization's repos stay separate. Their worktrees follow as `worktrees/<slug>/<owner>/<repo>`.
